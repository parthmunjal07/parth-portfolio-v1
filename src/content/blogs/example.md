## How to use this folder

1. Create a new `.md` file in the `src/content/blogs` folder.
2. At the very top of the file, include the `---` blocks (this is called frontmatter).
3. Fill in the `title`, `description`, `image`, and `date`.
4. Paste your Hashnode markdown content right below the second `---`.
5. Your blog will automatically show up on the website!
